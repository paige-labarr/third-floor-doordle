How to Play

Guess the 5-letter FLCC-themed word in 6 tries. Any 5-letter word (A–Z) is accepted so campus terms always count!

A new daily puzzle unlocks each morning at midnight Eastern Time from December 1 to December 18.

A Correct (✓): Letter is in the word and in the right spot.
B Misplaced (•): Letter is in the word, but in another spot.
C Not in Word: Letter does not appear in the answer.

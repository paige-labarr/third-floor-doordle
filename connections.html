How to Play Connections

Find four hidden groups of four words that share an FLCC or wordplay connection.

  • Select 4 tiles and tap Submit to check your group.
  • You have 4 mistakes per board. Duplicate guesses won’t cost a mistake.
  • Watch out for sneaky red herrings that seem to fit more than one category!
  • Official boards unlock at midnight EST on Dec 1, Dec 8, and Dec 15. Tap the week pill above the board to switch boards anytime.

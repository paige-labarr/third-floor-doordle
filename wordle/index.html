How to Play FLCC Wordle

Guess the hidden 5-letter FLCC word in 6 tries. Each guess can be any 5 letters (A–Z).

F L I C K

Green (Orange in High Contrast): Letter is in the word and in the right spot.

C A M P S

Yellow (Blue in High Contrast): Letter is in the word, but in a different spot.

S N O W Y

Gray: Letter is not in the word in any spot.

A new official puzzle unlocks daily from Dec 1–18 at midnight Eastern Time. Tap the date pill above the board anytime to switch between Practice Mode and unlocked days.

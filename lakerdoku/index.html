How to Play Lakerdoku

Fill every row, column, and bold-bordered 2×3 box with each symbol exactly once.

  • L-A-K-E-R-S or 1–6: Switch display modes anytime above the grid without losing progress (1=L, 2=A, 3=K, 4=E, 5=R, 6=S).
  • Notes Mode: Tap Notes (or press N) to jot down pencil candidates in empty cells. Placing a value automatically clears conflicting notes.
  • Conflict Detection: Duplicates in the same row, column, or 2×3 box show a red tint, a corner ! badge, and a wavy underline.
  • Weekly Unlocks: Official boards unlock at midnight EST on Dec 1 (Easy), Dec 8 (Medium), and Dec 15 (Challenging).
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys move, 1–6 or L A K E R S enter symbols, Backspace/0 erases, Z undoes.
